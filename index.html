Gratitude

First, I give all the glory to God.

Give thanks unto the lord, for he is good: for his mercy endureth for ever

Before the wishes, the celebrations, the dreams, and the plans for tomorrow — there is a reason to be grateful.

This is the day which the lord hath made; I will rejoice and be glad in it

God has been faithful through every season. For the gift of life, the privilege of another year, the lessons learned, the strength to keep moving, the people who have been part of my journey, and the opportunities still ahead, I give all the glory to Him.

I may not have everything figured out, and I may still be a work in progress, but I am grateful for how far I have come and hopeful about where God is leading me.

This new chapter is not just about getting older. It is about growing in wisdom, walking in purpose, becoming more disciplined, and using the gifts God has given me to make a meaningful difference.

Lord, thank You for another year. Let this next chapter bring greater wisdom, genuine growth, meaningful opportunities, and the grace to become who You have called me to be.

All glory belongs to You.
